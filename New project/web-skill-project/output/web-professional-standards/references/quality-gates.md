# Quality gates

Define the release scope and primary task. Prioritize by consequence independently of how much evidence you have. R0 is loss of a critical record, serious exposure, an irreversible wrong commitment or inability to complete the primary task, including through an accessibility barrier. R1 materially impairs use or maintenance with a viable workaround. R2 has limited impact.

| Surface touched | Evidence needed for this scope |
|---|---|
| Visitor or editor task | Correct route and real destination, usable content and keyboard-accessible action at relevant sizes and states. Confirm brand claims, contact values, assets and publication rights. Clear any draft labels on a production-critical path. |
| Durable action or restricted data | Qualified authorization, validation, expected authoritative state, truthful confirmation, safe repeat/recovery and evidence at any promised delivery boundary. |
| Public routing or release | Intended status, content and indexing on the actual target. For a migration, account for old links, compatible data, rollback or repair and an owner. |
| Ongoing operation | Responsible person can edit or operate the flow, recognize failure and recover. For critical records include evidence of restore or explicitly limit the handoff. |

For each material criterion record the route/state, environment/build, expected and observed outcome, method, limitation and owner. PASS requires positive observation. FAIL is a demonstrated mismatch. WARNING needs judgment. NOT TESTED identifies a missing observation. N/A needs the reason and does not count as a pass.

Release the affected production flow only with resolved R0s and completed mandatory evidence. For open R1s name consequence, workable alternative, owner and plan. An unresolved required warning needs a decision and evidence. When a service or device is unavailable, hand over the verified scope, such as a local preview, with a precise outstanding check. State what would change the decision.
